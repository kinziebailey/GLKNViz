The "Correlations" figure plots two parameters against each other. Each point on the figure is a pair of measurements taken on the same date at the same location. For the parameters that have depth profiles (e.g. water temperature, pH, speific conductivity, DO (mg/L), DO (%)), only data in the upper 2 meters of the water column are shown. If there is more than one value, the following rules are applied.

  1. The mean is calculated for two values
  2. The median is calculated for three or more values

The "Figure Data" tab shows a screen-reader-friendly version of the data used to create the "Correlations" figure. The "Exceedances" tab shows the data associated with any values that exceed the threshold values for the parameter of interest.

Downloads

You can download the figure in .png format by hovering the mouse over the figure and then clicking the download icon. You can view the figure in full screen by hovering the mouse over the figure and then clicking the full screen icon. For each table, you can download a .csv or .xlsx version of the data.

The "Depth Profiles" figure plots the depth profiles for one parameter for each site selected. GLKN only measures depth profiles for water temperature, pH, specific conductivity, dissolved oxygen concentration (mg/l), and dissolved oxygen saturation (%). Each row of the figure shows the profile plot for each month the parameter was measured. Each column of the figure shows the years selected in the section above. The "Figure Data" tab shows a screen-reader-friendly version of the data used to create the "Depth Profiles" figure. The "Exceedances" tab shows the data associated with any values that exceed the threshold values for the parameter of interest.

Water Quality Thresholds

This visualizer uses the general term “threshold” to broadly encompass criteria, standards, or thresholds to indicate when a parameter may be at levels detrimental to water quality. Checking the "Threshold" box will display this threshold as a line on the figure.

Downloads

You can download the figure in .png format by hovering the mouse over the figure and then clicking the download icon. You can view the figure in full screen by hovering the mouse over the figure and then clicking the full screen icon. For each table, you can download a .csv or .xlsx version of the data.

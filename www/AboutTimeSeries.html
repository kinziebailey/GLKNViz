The "Time Series" figure plots one parameter against time for each site selected. For the parameters that have depth profiles (e.g. water temperature, pH, specific conductivity, DO (mg/L), DO (%)), only data in the upper 2 meters of the water column are shown. If there is more than one value, the following rules are applied.

  1. The mean is calculated for two values
  2. The median is calculated for three or more values

The "Figure Data" tab shows a screen-reader-friendly version of the data use d to create the "Time Series" figure. The "Exceedances" tab shows the data associated with any values that exceed the threshold values for the parameter of interest.

Water Quality Thresholds

This visualizer uses the general term “threshold” to broadly encompass criteria, standards, or thresholds to indicate when a parameter may be at levels detrimental to water quality.. Checking the "Threshold" box will display this threshold as a line on the figure.
